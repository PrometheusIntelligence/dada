# Archive craft: building game content from a real archive

From The Holy Temple's regions drawn from the owner's PrometheusProtocol archive (THE INDEX, the four rooms, Foxbone Park),
2026-11-10..11. Lessons world L8, L25, L27-L29 are the evidence; this page is the method. Read it before any region that draws on real
records, real places, real programs or real people.

## The method

1. **Read the archive's own rules before its records.** PrometheusProtocol's README says it is "an index, not a finding": a solid edge copies
   a claim a record makes, a dashed edge only means two names share a list, a hedged row is a dotted co-mention, a denial makes no edge, and
   "a path is not proof". That rule was the best thing in the archive. It became the region's hub (Mrs. Knott's wall) and a paperwork game
   (the sorting table: CLAIM / LISTED / MENTION / NO LINE).
2. **Take a record's mechanism, not the archive as a whole.** One specific record per place, and the thing it does: a rule about not naming
   speakers (the Room Rule), a hotel suite where the same men met for decades (Room 8F), a retired officer approached to lead a takeover who
   testified and was denied (the Approach), a think tank that models everything (the Institute), a mole who left drops under a footbridge and
   hunted himself (Foxbone Park).
3. **A mechanism becomes a verb only when it changes what a key does** (world L29). The Room Rule's quotes are clues with the speaker blanked,
   and naming the speaker fails the move; the Institute's model makes its predicted move useless; the Index's rules decide how a card files.
   Flavour text alone is not a region.
4. **Tie it to the game's own people and institutions.** The strings run between the game's Host, Mayor, Department and Company, so the
   region links the world instead of the news (world L27). Every new place gets threads in and out (world L31, [world-ties.md](world-ties.md)).
5. **Keep every finding open.** Dossier entries stay NOT RESOLVED; cards file as what they say and no more; a man can be at the bridge and the
   card still files NO LINE. The game is about what people know and do not know (VISION 58).
6. **List every recognisable detail for the owner** (a famous number, a ritual, a method's name) and let the owner decide (world L25). Record
   the decision in VISION.md in their words, once, and follow it.

## What the owner decided (The Holy Temple)

| Decision | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| The island's blue-and-white striped temple stays | VISION 57 | a real place's look may be referenced; never "fix" an owner's deliberate reference |
| Direct references to real places, programs, rules and records are welcome | VISION 58 | "8F" kept; the originals' recognisable details may be named |
| A parody of a real public figure from the public record, when the owner asks by name | VISION 59 | ROBERT HANDSOME (after Robert Hanssen), FOXBONE PARK (after Foxstone Park): parody names, only the public record |
| Always out | the lead's standing limit | real people's private lives and families; anything touching sexual-abuse cases; no claims about living people beyond the public record |

Other owners decide differently: ask once, per game, and write the answer down (world L28's note). Without a decision, the default is the
strict one: structure and mechanism only, invented names, no real people.

## Checks

- [ ] The archive's README read; its own rule identified and used
- [ ] One named record per new place; its mechanism is a verb (it changes a key, a door, a standoff or a card)
- [ ] Strings run between the game's own institutions; the new region has threads in and out (`world_graph.py`)
- [ ] Every finding open (dossier NOT RESOLVED; cards file by the rules)
- [ ] Real-world details listed for the owner; the owner's decision quoted in VISION.md; the always-out list respected
