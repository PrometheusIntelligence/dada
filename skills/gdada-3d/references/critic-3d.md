# The 3D critic checklist (give this to the independent reader)

Look at stills from at least eight places per level: the arrival point, each wall, a doorway, a stair, a window, a corner, looking up, looking down.

1. **Does the space make sense?** Can you say where the stairs go, what each opening is, where the light comes from? Do openings have depth (reveals) or are they flat stickers?
2. **Proportions.** Doors, windows, furniture and stairs at plausible sizes against the room height and each other.
3. **Edges and depth.** Are there enough distinct edges per view? Is there a foreground, middle and background; do far things step down in tone; are there contact shadows?
4. **Smoothness.** Do round things look round (smooth columns, many-quad walls) or faceted?
5. **Style.** The chosen style's rules (cel: flat fills, two or three tones, ink edges, flat shadows; no dither).
6. **Readability.** Dark on dark; props the same colour as the wall; labels that cover the thing; clipped HUD; near-plane wedges.
7. **Mood.** Does the room carry the game's mood contract with light and props, one wrong thing at a time?
8. **Say what you did not do:** read, not played; no motion; no timing.

Output: a table (view | observed in which still | PASS/OBJECT | change) and a ranked top five.
